How it works

What happens in a tenth of a second.

Spacewalk lets the Dock do the switching, so macOS stays consistent. It only replaces what you see while it happens.

Freeze

The last frame from a capture stream covers the screen.

12–18 ms

Switch

A Dock swipe with nothing left to animate commits the switch.

30–140 ms

Capture

The first new frame becomes the destination.

10–25 ms

Animate

Core Animation plays your transition, then the overlay hides.

Everything else

Made for people who live in Spaces.

Follow your fingers

Three-finger swipes play your transition and track the gesture. A haptic tick marks the point where lifting will switch.

Overview

Every Space as a live picture with its name and app icons. Arrows, digits, typing or a click take you there.

Shortcuts

⌃1 to ⌃9, next, previous, back and forth, or take over ⌃← and ⌃→. Bind a key to an app to jump to its Space.

Names and a menu bar

Name your Spaces. The menu bar shows where you are, and an optional bar lists every Space with its apps.

A config file

Every setting lives in ~/.config/spacewalk/config.toml, reloaded when you save it.

A command line

spacewalk next, spacewalk effect cube, spacewalk overview. Script anything.

Install

Three ways in.

Homebrew

Installs the app and puts the spacewalk command on your PATH.

brew install --cask tornikegomareli/tap/spacewalk

Terminal

Downloads the latest notarized release, checks it with Gatekeeper and installs it. No sudo.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/InsaneArts/Spacewalk/main/install.sh | bash

Download

The disk image from the latest release. Drag Spacewalk to Applications.

Download Spacewalk.dmg

Needs macOS 26.6 or later on Apple Silicon, and two permissions it asks for on first launch: Screen Recording, to see your Spaces, and Accessibility, to switch them. Updates arrive in the app, signed and verified.
