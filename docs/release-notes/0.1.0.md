First release.

- Twelve transitions between Spaces: instant, slide, depth, tilt, carousel, fade, zoom, cube, flip, swap, reveal, stack. Live preview in Settings.
- Hotkeys for Spaces 1 to 9, next, previous and back-and-forth, plus optional takeover of ⌃← and ⌃→.
- Trackpad: three-finger swipes play the transition, or follow your fingers with a haptic tick at the point of no return.
- Overview: every Space as a live picture with its name and app icons; keyboard, mouse, typing or the Mission Control swipe pick one.
- Per-Space wallpapers, Space names, menu bar indicator, Spaces bar, mouse side buttons, jump-to-app hotkeys, multi-display follow.
- Settings in `~/.config/spacewalk/config.toml`, reloaded when saved; `spacewalk` CLI.
- Automatic updates through Sparkle.
